# Stewardship

## Stewardship Model

Open Operational State uses a stewardship model rather than a traditional maintainer hierarchy. Stewards are responsible for the health, direction, and integrity of the initiative.

## Steward Responsibilities

Stewards are expected to:

- **Uphold scope discipline** — enforce v1 scope boundaries and resist scope creep
- **Maintain architectural integrity** — ensure changes align with the locked six-layer architecture
- **Review contributions** — review PRs for quality, scope, neutrality, and alignment
- **Maintain neutrality** — ensure all assets remain genuinely vendor-neutral
- **Advance the roadmap** — drive progress through the phased roadmap
- **Document decisions** — record significant decisions as ADRs
- **Foster community** — create a welcoming, professional environment for contributors

## Becoming a Steward

In this early phase, stewards are the founding contributors. As the project matures, additional stewards may be invited based on:

- Sustained, high-quality contributions
- Demonstrated understanding of the architecture and principles
- Commitment to neutrality and scope discipline
- Community trust

The process for adding stewards will be formalized as the governance model evolves.

## Steward Conduct

Stewards are held to the same [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/open-operational-state/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) as all contributors, with the additional expectation that they model the behavior they expect from others.

## Stepping Down

Stewards may step down at any time by notifying other active stewards. Orderly transition of responsibilities is expected.
